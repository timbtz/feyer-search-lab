    Where does everything run?

    Each participant

    A local checkout, personal Claude or Codex account, and owned research branches.

    Optional Hetzner services

    A broker or selection helper can run remotely. Host placement is subject to change; colleagues do not need VPS access.

    Each AMD instance

    One serial evaluation queue, the fixed benchmark environment, and a separately calibrated baseline.

    GitHub

    Source commits, owned branches, hypotheses, and published results.

    What improved?

    Compare complete first-run time. A correct result is not automatically a faster one.

    Times are medians of recorded full runs where available. Single measurements need confirmation. Comparisons stay within the same hardware and validation cohort.

    How we judge an improvement

    Primary objective

    Minimize complete first-run time: imports and setup, compilation, forward simulation, sensitivity, gradients, and result transfer.

    Useful tradeoffs

    Compilation, execution time, and peak memory also form a Pareto comparison. A gain in one can come with a cost in another. Exact Pareto membership and improvement beyond measurement noise are recorded separately.

    Correctness first

    Screening is provisional. Full acceptance requires the official benchmark and our separate saved-output and all-gradient comparison to pass.

    Where the time goes.

    Recorded benchmark phases for one exact job. First-run, warm timing, and job wall time remain separate.

    Does it hold beyond the benchmark?

    Additional setup and transformation checks are internal review evidence, separate from official benchmark acceptance. Each candidate is compared with the unchanged simulator on other setups (same three-stage pipeline, outputs and gradient) and under other JAX transformations.
