    Compare complete first-run time. A correct result is not automatically a faster one.

    Times are medians of recorded full runs where available. Single measurements need confirmation. Comparisons stay within the same hardware and validation cohort.

    How we judge an improvement

    Primary objective

    Minimize complete first-run time: imports and setup, compilation, forward simulation, sensitivity, gradients, and result transfer.

    Useful tradeoffs

    Compilation, execution time, and peak memory also form a Pareto comparison. A gain in one can come with a cost in another. Exact Pareto membership and improvement beyond measurement noise are recorded separately.

    Correctness first

    Screening is provisional. Full acceptance requires the official benchmark and our separate saved-output and all-gradient comparison to pass.
